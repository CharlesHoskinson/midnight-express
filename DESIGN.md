# Midnight Express visual identity

Express follows the [Midnight Brand Hub](https://midnight.network/brand-hub): Midnight Black `#0A0A0A`, white `#FFFFFF`, electric blue `#0000FE`, and Outfit. Outfit is served locally with its SIL Open Font License. Brand blue carries the monogram, primary actions and selected navigation. Small text and focus indicators use a lighter blue to retain contrast against black.

The site serves people reviewing a protocol and developers operating a subscription prototype, often in a dark desktop workspace. Near-black is the page ground; panels stay charcoal with thin neutral borders. White sets the reading hierarchy. Blue marks a real action, selection or technical connection. Existing mint, teal and amber accents are removed. Branding changes apply to Overview, Data model, Subscriptions, Specification and Implementation.

The wordmark reads Midnight Express with a compact M/E monogram. It identifies this project without altering Midnight’s official logo. Headers, controls and prose use Outfit; code and payloads retain monospace. Decorative counting and eyebrow labels are excluded.

Generated imagery depicts sealed events and proposed component relationships. It supplies visual explanation, not implementation evidence. Diagrams have equivalent HTML explanations and full-size links; operational controls remain native HTML. The subscription workspace has no decorative image competing with its directory.

Asset prompts and provenance are recorded in `design/branding/generated-assets.json`. Brand research and retrieval limits are in `design/branding/reference/source-notes.json`. The palette is verified from the official Brand Hub, rather than inferred from a failed browser-verification screen.
