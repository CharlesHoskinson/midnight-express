## Type
- Display and headings: **Iosevka Charon**, uppercase, tight tracking (about -2 px at 56 px), regular weight.
- Body: **Inter**. Labels and numbers: Iosevka Charon or Geist Mono, small, letter-spaced, uppercase.
- Code: Geist Mono.

## Motifs
- Pure black ground; panels with hairline borders and small notched (chevron) corners; orange chevron-bracket buttons and rules.
- Small eyebrow labels with a diamond mark in blue, green or orange above headings.
- Pixel-art scenes: night skylines, cliffs and clouds in deep blue, warm orange window light, vignette to black.
- Diagrams: pixel or blueprint style on black, hairline panels, thin blue lines, orange for the path that matters.

## Print translation
Cover, part dividers, appendix dividers and figure panels are dark and full-bleed. Body pages are white with near-black text (#0b1220), orange
rules, blue links (#1f5fa8), headings in Iosevka Charon uppercase, chevron-notched call-out boxes, eyebrow labels for chapter and part.
