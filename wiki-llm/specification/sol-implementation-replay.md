# Structural replay implementation

`MidnightExpress/Replay.lean` adds `Journal`, `structuralReplay`, and the public `structuralCheck c s e`. Journal occurrences bind full decoded `Event` values; actions bind `ActionKey` to full `BusinessIntent`. `Event.actionKey?` derives the key from approval content. Replay accepts no caller-supplied occurrence or intent digests and uses no hash-injectivity premise. The historical token-based `ReplayState` / `check` remain available in `Validation.lean` for compatibility.

Current-context validation still runs first. Identical occurrences return `duplicateEvent`; fresh occurrences with identical bound business content return `duplicateAction`; changed occurrence content or changed bound action content reject. `structural_changed_approval_expiry_conflicts` states the concrete expiry-change case. Conflict properties cover arbitrary journals with an explicit fresh occurrence premise, or all occurrences in coherent/reachable journals.

`Journal.WellFormed` requires unique occurrence/action keys, matching occurrence keys, every approval occurrence's action/content binding, and an occurrence witness for every action binding. Successful checks preserve it; `Reachable` starts at the empty journal and implies it. Lookup bindings are immutable across successful steps and arbitrary runs. In a coherent journal even a duplicate approval occurrence has its action bound, addressing the malformed-initial-state retry counterexample.

`run s trace` threads the journal through `(Context × Event)` attempts and returns `(List (Event × Option Result), Journal)`. Rejections preserve the state. The context can change independently at each step. `run_at_most_one_fresh_candidate` proves that `candidateCount` for any action is at most one across the entire trace, even from an arbitrary initial journal; `structural_no_second_candidate_across_run` supplies the explicit first-candidate/intervening-run/later-check corollary. These are classification guarantees, not effect commitment or durable accounting guarantees.

Validation completed with the pinned Lean 4.34.1 command `lake --wfail build MidnightExpress.Replay`. A separate kernel-checked scratch regression at `/tmp/midnight-structural-replay-tests.lean` exercised first acceptance, exact retry, fresh occurrence for the same action, currently valid changed approval expiry, revocation, expiry, and a five-attempt mixed trace. Its `by decide` cases pass. Representative theorem dependency reports contain only Lean's `propext` and `Quot.sound`, with no project axioms or proof placeholders. Repository-wide asserting audits and stateful fixture vectors are owned by the integration/bridge work.
