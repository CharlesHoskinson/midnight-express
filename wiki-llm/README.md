# Midnight Express research wiki

This wiki stores source evidence, research approaches, intermediate findings, alternatives and decisions for the subscription design. It contains concise research notes and decision rationales. Private model reasoning is not archived. Raw captures remain distinct from adopted product requirements.

Start with [the pub/sub research](pubsub/README.md). Agent reports record provider/model provenance and link retrieved primary sources. Fetch failures and visual-capture limitations remain visible. A recommendation does not become an implemented capability until the relevant artifact and acceptance evidence exist.

[Specification research](specification/) records the Astra modeling, validation and reader-guidance work. The [Lean project](../formal/lean/README.md) defines its proof scope and reproduction commands.
