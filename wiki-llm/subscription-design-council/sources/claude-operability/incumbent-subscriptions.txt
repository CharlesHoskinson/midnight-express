Inbox and quarantine
Delivery cursor tracks local receipt; processing cursor tracks contiguous decided dispositions (reviewed or quarantined). Neither proves an action ran or consensus finality. Agent mode prepares no tools or effects. Bounds: two in-flight items, four queued events and 16 KiB per subscription. Backpressure stops scheduling without dropping undecided work.
