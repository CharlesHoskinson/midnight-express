How to find and add/follow sources?
You can find and follow new sources in the discover page that can be reached via the RSS icon button in the laft navigation bar. 
On top of the discover page, you will find a search box allowing you to search for any topic, hashtag, website name or URL.
Tip: try the SIMILAR SOURCES link to find similar sources to those you like
