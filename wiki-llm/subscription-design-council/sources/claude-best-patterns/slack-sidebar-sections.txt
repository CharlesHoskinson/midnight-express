Organize your sidebar with custom sections
Organize your sidebar with custom sections
If you use Slack on a 
paid plan
, you can organize your channels, direct messages (DMs), and apps into custom sections within your sidebar. Your custom sections are only visible to you and won't affect what your coworkers see, so set them up however you’d like.
Note:
 If you’re using the free version of Slack, read about ways to 
manage what's in your sidebar
 or 
start a free trial
 to use custom sections.
Create custom sections
Desktop
Mobile
From the 
 Home
 tab, hover over the 
 Channels
 section in your sidebar. 
Click the 
 three dots icon
.
Click 
 Channel section
.
Enter a name for your custom section, or choose one of the suggestions.
Click the 
 smiley face icon
 to select an emoji to display next to your section.
Click 
Create
.
Open a channel or DM you'd like to create a new section for.
Tap the channel or member name(s) in the conversation header.
Tap the 
 Star 
(if you have no existing sections) or 
Move 
button (if you have sections).
Tap 
 New Section
.
Enter a name for your custom section, or choose one of the suggestions.
Tap the 
 smiley face icon
 to select an emoji to display next to your section.
Tap 
Create
.
Tip:
Share a sidebar section
 with others to invite them to multiple channels at once.
Organize your conversations
Move a conversation to a section
Desktop
Mobile
You can drag and drop any conversation to a section from your sidebar, or follow the steps below to move a conversation you're viewing to a section:
Open a channel or DM.
Click the 
 star icon
 (or the emoji associated with the custom section) at the top of the conversation.
Select a custom section from the list, or click 
 Create new section
 to create a new one.
Open the channel or DM you'd like to move.
Tap the channel or member name(s) in the conversation header.
Tap 
 Star
 (if you have no existing sections) or 
Move
 (if you have sections).
Select a custom section from the list, or tap 
 New Section
 to create a new one.
Bulk manage conversations
Move multiple channels or DMs into a section at once. Add them to an existing section or create a new one.
From the top of the  
 Home 
tab on desktop, click the 
 Manage my sidebar 
icon.
Click 
 Bulk manage conversations
.
Check or uncheck the boxes next to the conversations you want to move.
Click 
Move to…
.
Select a custom section. If you select the section the conversations are already in, it will remove them from that section.
You can also click 
 Create new section 
to move selected channels to a new custom section. Add a name and optional emoji, confirm your preferred sorting, then click 
Create and Add Channels
.
Click the 
 checkmark icon 
when you are done making changes.
Browse or search a section
Find channels that have been 
filtered from view
, including any inactive, 
muted
, or read conversations.
Desktop
Mobile
From the 
 Home
 tab, hover over any custom section name in your sidebar.
Click the 
 search icon 
for more conversations.
Type the name of the channel you are looking for, or scroll through the full list.
Tap the 
 Home
 tab at the bottom of your screen.
Tap the custom section you want to browse.
Scroll through the channels in that section.
Remove a conversation from a section
When you remove a conversation from a custom section, it’ll move back to the 
 Channels
 or 
 Direct messages
 section of your sidebar.
Desktop
Mobile
Open a channel or DM.
Click the 
 star icon
 (or the emoji associated with the custom section) at the top of the conversation.
Select 
Remove from [section name]
.
Open the channel or DM you'd like to remove.
Tap the channel or member name(s) in the conversation header.
Tap 
Move
.
Tap 
 Remove from [section name]
.
Note:
 In an Enterprise organization, a channel will only appear in a custom section in your sidebar if it’s been 
added to the workspace
 you’re viewing.
Rename or delete custom sections
You can rename a custom section or update the associated emoji at any time. If you no longer need a custom section, you can delete it.
Desktop
Mobile
From the 
 Home
 tab, select any custom section name in your sidebar.
Click the 
 three dots icon
.
Click 
Rename 
or 
Delete section
.
Tap the 
 Home
 tab at the bottom of your screen.
Tap a custom section name.
Tap the 
 three dots icon 
in the top right corner.
Tap 
Rename [section name]
 or 
Delete [section name]
.
Manage conversation display
If you’d like, you can override the 
display and sorting preferences
 you’ve set for your entire sidebar by adjusting them for a specific custom section. Each section can have its own filters and sorting.
Filter a custom section
From the 
 Home
 tab, hover over any custom section name in your sidebar.
Click the 
 three dots
 icon.
Hover over 
Filter
 to choose how you'd like to filter your conversations. By default, you'll see 
Active only
, which will hide conversations with no new activity in the past 30 days. 
Sort a custom section
From the 
Home
 tab, hover over any custom section name in your sidebar.
Click the 
 three dots icon
.
Hover over 
Sort
 to choose how you’d like to sort your conversations: alphabetically (
A-Z
), by 
Recency
, or by 
Priority
.
