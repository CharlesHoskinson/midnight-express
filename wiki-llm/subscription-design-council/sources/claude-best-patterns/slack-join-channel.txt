Join a channel
Slack organizes conversations in your workspace into dedicated spaces called 
channels
. Channels bring the right people, tools, and information together for a common purpose — like working on a specific project, or discussing a particular topic with your team. Keep reading to learn how to find and join channels in your workspace.
Tip: 
If you've been invited to work with another company in a channel using Slack Connect, learn how to 
accept the invitation
.
