How to use Team Feeds and Team Boards
When you’re using Feedly as part of an enterprise team, it helps to understand how your 
team feeds
, 
boards
, and 
personal feeds
 work together, and why organizing them well makes your workflow smoother.
Team Feeds
What they are:
Team Feeds automatically update as new content is published. They are organized into Folders, which you can expand or collapse. Team Feeds include:
AI Feeds
:
 Powered by AI Models and filters to surface high-signal intel.
RSS Feeds
: Custom sources you or your team have added.
Best practice:
Keep 
AI feeds
 and 
RSS feeds
 in 
separate folders
. For example, you might prefix AI feed folders with “AI” to make them easy to spot.
➜ Why? AI Feeds automatically filter out noise and surface relevant intel. RSS feeds pull in 
everything,
 so mixing them can muddy the signal.
How to use:
Click a 
folder
 to see all articles from feeds inside it at once.
Or drill down into a specific AI feed if you want to focus on that topic only.
Team Boards
What they are:
Boards are where you 
manually save
 and 
organize
 articles that matter most. They’re perfect for:
Highlighting priority intel for the team.
Building curated content for a newsletter or briefing.
Adding 
analyst notes
 or context for others.
How to use:
Save an article from any feed to a board.
Add comments or notes for your teammates.
Tag people, assign follow-ups, or even share directly to Slack or email — right from Feedly.
Personal Feeds & Boards
What they are:
Personal feeds and boards are 
private,
 only you can see them.
When to use them:
Researching sensitive topics that shouldn’t be shared with the full team yet.
Working on a confidential request from leadership.
Tracking personal interests or side projects (yes, you can track hobby topics too!).
