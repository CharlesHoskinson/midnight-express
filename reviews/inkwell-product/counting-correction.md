# Counting correction

The previous editorial pass preserved the website’s numbered section labels, component badges, card ranks and journey counters. It also retained portfolio counts and test totals in product explanations. Those features kept the counted-tour structure the user wanted removed.

Section labels now name their subjects. Prototype navigation uses Quote exchange, Recovery and Workflow reuse, while the existing anchors remain stable. Card ranks and lettered pilot labels are removed by the generator itself; the canonical ranking data remains available for product requirements. Journey controls use their action names. Data-model evidence describes accepted interpretation, transfer replay and report-write recovery without using test totals as the explanation. Technical results remain in their original evidence artifacts.

Price arithmetic, proof limits, version identifiers, business approval thresholds and protocol requirements remain intact. The public quantity examples retain their declared units and conversions. Current guides remove counted titles and numbered subject headings. The website maintenance guide records this editorial convention for future edits.

Browser verification covers the changed controls, card disclosure layout, mobile/enlarged text and reading without JavaScript. Canonical use-case and sprint registers and normative obligations are unchanged. This is a prose and presentation correction; it adds no protocol capability or customer evidence.
