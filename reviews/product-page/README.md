# Eight product-manager reviews of the architecture website

Eight agents reviewed the published GitHub Pages content, its local HTML/JavaScript and the canonical product recommendation. Their HTTP/source reviews are not customer research or evidence of production readiness.

| Review | Change applied |
|---|---|
| Positioning | Plain-language definition, explicit audience and first proposed pilot. |
| Information flow | Definition → problem → illustrative workflows → use cases → architecture → delivery. Existing architecture and journey anchors retained. |
| Business value | Concrete problems, baseline-based evaluation, no invented savings or demand. |
| Financial workflows | Requests-for-quotes and invoice stories; initial coordination scope separated from settlement/payment execution. |
| Agents/governance | Scoped human approvals, issuer freshness, explicit threshold policy and application responsibility. |
| Enterprise use cases | Actor, trigger and illustrative scenario for procurement, insurance, incidents, risk and contract notifications. |
| Trust/readiness | Midnight's role, metadata/bandwidth tradeoffs, current foundations and remaining gates. |
| Reader comprehension | Glossary, FAQ, main-content skip link, static use-case content and a quote example available without JavaScript. |

The detailed Umbra section and its generated illustration are retained after the product story, with recovery explained in workflow terms. Readiness is summarized in the FAQ and delivery section rather than adding a duplicative capability matrix. The ranking and stable requirement IDs are unchanged; editorial examples are marked illustrative and do not extend the canonical requirements.

Validation: JavaScript syntax, local references and generated card IDs/ranks; headless Chromium desktop/mobile interaction checks, keyboard focus after journey navigation, three business workflows, nine component selectors, ten native use-case cards, 200% text without horizontal overflow/clipping, no script exceptions, and the core story/cards with JavaScript disabled. These checks validate the page, not the Express protocol or business hypotheses.
