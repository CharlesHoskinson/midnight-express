# Sprint 3: invoice reuse and bounded human-approved sandbox actions

Status: proposed design and acceptance plan, 2026-10-03. No runtime changes, executed tests or delivered integrations are reported here. This expands [Sprint 3](sequence.md) using [Sprint 2 recovery](recovery.md), the canonical [first three use cases](../../docs/product-requirements/recommended-stack-and-use-cases.md), and the [domain](../data-model/domain-modelling.md), [financial](../data-model/financial-semantics.md) and [agent safety](../data-model/safe-agent-actions.md) reviews.

## Goal, entry and boundaries

Prove that the RFQ event/recovery core supports UC-03 invoice/payment-status reconciliation and UC-05 human approval with two distinct installed domain profiles. Deliver one synthetic invoice/payment adapter and one sandbox ERP destination. The agent proposes exactly one typed operation, `PostInvoiceMatchV1`, against that destination; a human signs its exact intent before a dedicated executor can post. An agent may be a deterministic fixture. LLM integration, real bank connections and unrestricted tool execution do not establish reuse and are unnecessary for this sprint.

Entry requires Sprint 2's verified public transaction composition, durable action guards, fault evidence, supported contract bundles, single-writer enforcement and destination-idempotency interface. Fix failed core cases before adding these adapters. The Node >=24 host uses UmbraDB/PostgreSQL through one public transaction handle; `saveAndAdvance` alone remains insufficient and must not be nested. The future atomic MPE helper remains unavailable. SQLite client behavior needs separate evidence. Admission, authoritative finalized Registry roots and original wire conformance remain independently reported gates; a mock-supported internal workflow demo cannot pass them.

All amounts represent synthetic accounting units. There is no payment instruction, funds transfer, settlement, production ERP posting, purchase or real-money execution. `PostInvoiceMatchV1` means recording a sandbox allocation under accounting policy, never marking a bank payment executed. No arbitrary shell, SQL, HTTP, file path or generic tool/parameter map is executable. Credentials stay with the narrow sandbox adapter, outside agent/model input. Do not imply production readiness, exactly-once arbitrary external execution, replicated retention, availability or measured customer savings.

## Reuse contract and identifier invariants

Keep the minimal event core unchanged: authenticated event identity, exact semantic-contract references, workflow/correlation/causation and the agreed time/expiry conventions. Money, payment states, approval budgets and invoice totals belong to separate domain profiles and reusable pinned value types. Do not infer a profile from payload shape or create a universal `completed` state.

| Shared component reused from RFQ | Domain-owned addition |
|---|---|
| Rust sealing/recognition, encrypted CloudEvents-compatible payload and TypeScript SDK | `invoice.reconcile.v1` and `agent.approval.v1` installed closed bundles |
| Authenticated inbox, original bytes and signature evidence, event guard | Invoice/source mapping and agent purpose/role checks |
| Source version/gap handling and exact bundle dispatch | Invoice revision, payment finality/reversal rules and approval-policy epoch |
| Atomic local projection/action guard/outbox/checkpoint/cursor composition | Allocation eligibility; approval consumption and budget reservation |
| Stable destination action key, conditional dispatch, query/reconciliation | Typed sandbox ERP operation and trusted approval renderer |
| Protected quarantine, bounded retry, authorized redrive and fault harness | Domain rejection reasons and human-resolution screens |

Pin core, profile, workflow/policy, code-set and adapter digests separately from the outer wire/security profile. Retain older installed validators throughout the replay/restore horizon. Domain review names exact event tags; the invoice vocabulary here specializes the review's notice/observation/allocation family. Agent proposal/approval/request/result tags below refine its broader lifecycle family. There are no interchangeable aliases or runtime version fallbacks. Denial and revocation are their own closed policy events; lifecycle observations cannot substitute for any authority assertion.

Separate identifiers explicitly:

- Envelope EID identifies sealed bytes. Retransmission preserves sealed bytes, EID and transport expiry; resealing produces a different EID, without renewing business validity or consumption.
- `(authenticated source, event id)` identifies one immutable event and occurrence digest. The same identity with changed content conflicts. Source record identity/revision and workflow identity are separate.
- `effectId` is the stable business-action identity at `(application/network/tenant authority domain, destination replay domain, effectId)`. Bind it to the exact canonical authorized-intent digest and destination result. Do not namespace consumption by EID, event ID, schema version or policy epoch in a way that renews the action.
- A new event/new EID carrying a consumed effect with matching digest returns the prior authenticated result, or reconciles its unfinished outcome. A different digest with the same key conflicts. Cross-profile re-emission shares the guard rather than dual-writing the destination.
- `allocationId`, `proposalId`, `approvalId`, `requestId` and source payment IDs identify their respective records. Relationships are explicit signed references, not guesses based on correlation or invoice number alone.

All business selectors, approval references and source/finality fields stay inside encryption. Whole-shard/window retrieval and local recognition remain unchanged; no invoice/agent topics or receiver-interest filters. Protect source documents, approval assertions, database/WAL/backups and quarantine within the endpoint trust domain. Shareable logs contain redacted decision counts, never plaintext invoice/account/target routing headers.

## Invoice adapter and guarded state machine

Use one supplier-scoped invoice for synthetic USD 100.00, represented as coefficient `10000`, scale `2`, with purchaser and supplier namespace/scheme/value identities, revision `1`, document digest, issue/due accounting dates and payable total. The fixture uses a restricted UBL concept mapping; it is not a UBL-conformance claim. One currency and the pinned payable-total/rounding convention are supported. Reject multi-currency, unsupported fees/tax/corrections, lost economic fields or missing account facts rather than inventing conversion rules. Preserve original document bytes/digest. Corrections and credit notes create linked revisions/events; they do not overwrite original signed evidence.

The adapter manifest pins source system/authority, schema/status vocabulary version, adapter digest, permitted signer roles, account/rail identities, payment identity, source revision/base, as-of/effective time, currency/scale, payer/payee, finality and reversal link mappings. An adapter signature authenticates a source assertion, not actual bank settlement. The demo uses explicitly signed synthetic evidence; a production source would need independently accepted authentication and finality policy. Unknown source, stale revision, missing predecessor or unsupported status yields a visible insufficient-evidence/gap disposition.

| Event/decision | Guard and permitted state |
|---|---|
| `InvoiceNotice/v1` | Authorized issuer, exact scoped identity/document/revision, supported monetary totals. Create `open`; do not infer payment from invoice issuance. Conflicting supplier/document identity quarantines. |
| `PaymentStatusObserved/v1`, provisional | Authenticate source and subject; retain observation/revision as `provisional`. It may produce a suggested match, never confirmed allocation, posting eligibility or `reconciled` status. |
| Payment evidence eligible under pinned finality policy | Require accepted source revision, exact payer/payee/account/rail linkage, supported currency/scale and available amount. No source gap or unresolved reversal. Eligible evidence can support `AllocationProposed/v1`; it cannot directly execute a posting. |
| `AllocationConfirmed/v1` | Authorized accounting decision pins invoice revision/digest, payment identity/revision/digest, exact allocation amount and destination. CAS invoice/payment allocation totals, prevent reuse of the same payment balance and enforce total bounds. Confirmation establishes eligibility for a separate posting action. |
| Final partial payment USD 40.00 | Permit a 40.00 allocation, leaving 60.00 outstanding. State is `partiallyReconciled` only after the declared posting/evidence conditions hold; never `paid`/fully reconciled. Wrong or unallocated amount never closes the invoice. |
| Final additional payment USD 60.00 | May close the outstanding balance only after exact allocation, posting confirmations and unchanged invoice/evidence revisions. `reconciled` means this profile's accounting predicate, not independently verified bank settlement. |
| Overpayment, wrong currency, fees/refund, ambiguous invoice linkage | Preserve evidence and enter bounded human review. No guessing invoice, silently netting fees or applying an unexplained remainder. |
| New reversal/retraction linked to an observation/allocation | Mark affected evidence ineligible and allocation `reversalPendingReview`; recompute outstanding balance from eligible allocations. Block any unsent posting dependent on it. Keep committed posting and audit history; do not erase, blindly undo or create a compensation grant. |
| Correction or revision changes after approval | Bound revision/digest precondition fails at execution. Refresh allocation/intent and obtain fresh approval for changed meaning. |

For a finalized-but-later-reversed source claim, the reversal rule still applies: finality is a source/profile predicate, not permission to suppress later contradictory evidence. A reversal received after an ERP posting requires a separately identified and authorized adjustment outside this sprint's automatic action set. If the destination outcome is unknown, reconcile first; record both reversal and execution uncertainty. The UI must not display full reconciliation while an eligible allocation lacks required posting confirmation, a source gap exists, or a reversal is unresolved.

All invoice evidence/allocation processing runs through the shared inbox and local transaction. Allocation confirmation and action preparation can be distinct domain transitions, each with atomic guards/outbox/progress. Source timestamps never replace authoritative revision ordering. Snapshot/delta repair must be source-authorized; an older snapshot cannot remove a newer reversal or guard.

## Exact bounded agent authority

Install four mutually exclusive contracts with literal tags, closed nested objects and separate signing-purpose constants:

| Contract | Meaning and allowed behavior |
|---|---|
| `ActionProposal/v1` | Proposer asserts exact typed intent/evidence. Store, validate and display only; no effect or implicit capability. |
| `ActionApproval/v1` | Authorized human signs the exact proposal and action digests with restricted scope. Store grant; it is neither an execution request nor success evidence. |
| `ExecutionRequest/v1` | Authenticated actor requests the exact approved operation. The executor rechecks current policy and may reserve/dispatch. |
| `ActionResult/v1` | Executor reports request/action/approval identities, destination evidence and explicit outcome/finality. It grants no retry, compensation or further capability. |

The single action is `PostInvoiceMatchV1`: invoice issuer/ID/revision/digest, allocation ID/digest, payment evidence digest/revision/finality, ledger/target record/revision, executor/destination, asset/currency, exact amount coefficient/scale and debit/credit interpretation. The intent also binds effect ID, operation and contract digest, deployment/network/tenant authority domain, authorized actor, policy/capability digest and epoch, target identity, validity interval, maximum total budget and maximum effect count `1`. The demo's maximum budget is a synthetic 40.00 posting with no fees; any adapter that cannot bound total cost or define fees is unavailable. The budget is a limit on the declared sandbox posting operation, not permission to spend real USD. Aggregate policy reservations are scoped by tenant/actor/policy/asset/period and enforced separately from per-action limits.

Pin an exact canonical input grammar: bounded decimal-string coefficients, fixed asset-specific scale, no coercion/floating-point/default insertion, a fixed UTC instant representation and exclusive expiry. Normalize representational equivalence into the profile's unique decimal representation before producing signed intent, and reject noncanonical actionable input. Reject duplicate decoded keys before map deserialization, invalid Unicode, excessive nesting/length and unknown nested members. Install schema closure and references locally; no runtime schema/URL/policy fetch. Cross-language vectors must agree on canonical bytes and rendered values.

Use the safety review's proposed digest construction with separately published purpose labels: `SHA-256(label || uint64_be(byte_length) || JCS_UTF8(assertion))`; signatures are outside their own preimages. Pin algorithm/key selection to the local authority allowlist. The proposal signs its exact intent and evidence commitments; approval signs proposal/action digests, approval ID, approving principal/role, actor, target/executor/destination, policy identity, validity and single-use scope. References to artifacts bind immutable content digests and revision; mutable URL contents never supply authorized terms.

A trusted approval screen renders the verified typed operation, exact invoice/supplier/destination, amount/currency/scale/direction, budget, fees convention, expiry, actor/executor and evidence limitations. Advisory agent/source text is escaped and labeled separately. Sign only those verified canonical bytes. Editing any target, amount, input or restriction creates a new intent and fresh approval. A valid seal, key possession, admission proof or anchoring supplies no execution capability. Check approver role rights, delegation attenuation, separation of duties and revocation independently of signature validity.

At the effect boundary, require accepted policy digest plus current stricter restrictions; stale or incompatible policy needs reapproval. Recheck invoice/evidence/target revisions, current revocation and controlled-clock validity before a genuinely new send. Approval valid on receipt but expired in queue must fail. `now == expiresAt` is expired; conservative clock uncertainty and offline behavior are pinned in the manifest. Atomically reserve budget and consume single-use action/approval under guarded updates; concurrent individually valid requests cannot oversubscribe aggregate budget. Timeout does not release reservations while an effect may have happened.

## Shared transaction, dispatch and unknown outcomes

1. Persist authenticated original input in the common inbox. Validate installed profile and domain semantics; preserve unsupported/conflicting evidence in protected quarantine. Retention receipt is not processing or completion.
2. Within the shared public `withTransaction`, resolve event/effect guards, CAS source/domain revisions, record approved allocation or approval/request state, reserve applicable budget, stage immutable outbox intent/result, checkpoint and advance only contiguous processing dispositions. Rollback removes all these changes. Do not execute remote work inside the callback.
3. A persisted execution request/reservation means `accepted`, not `committed` at the ERP. Immediately before dispatch apply the current effect-boundary policy/revision/expiry checks. Accepted cancellation/revocation prevents a new send; an already-linearized destination effect cannot be retroactively prevented. The sandbox destination should support an atomic deadline/revision check at its effect point; otherwise declare narrower gateway send-time semantics and test the limitation rather than imply destination-time revocation.
4. Dispatch only the installed typed operation using stable destination key plus canonical action digest. The sandbox destination durably commits key/digest/result/posting atomically, conflicts on changed digest and provides authenticated lookup. Track `dispatched`, `outcomeUnknown`, `committed` and, only if supported, `finalized` distinctly. MPE acknowledgement or agent “done” cannot promote them.
5. After timeout/crash/lost response, query the exact destination key. Prior success records the same result and budget consumption; authoritative nonexecution permits policy-rechecked retry with the same key/digest. Unavailable/inconclusive lookup leaves uncertainty and reserved budget intact. A query resolving an old effect is distinct from authorization to perform a new effect after expiry.
6. For a destination lacking trustworthy idempotency and query/reconciliation, disable automatic effectful execution. The negative fixture shows manual review after ambiguity and zero blind resend. Stable-key retry is safe only under the declared durable destination contract; a local transaction alone cannot guarantee a remote outcome.
7. Restore/replay retains guards and reservations from authoritative storage. Older checkpoints cannot resurrect approvals, roll back reversal state or release unknown reservations. Full backup rollback without trusted freshness blocks automatic effect recovery. Redrive is explicit, bounded and repeats current authority checks without renewed IDs/expiry.

After a destination success plus lost local confirmation, expiry or revocation does not erase the prior success. Reconcile and record it without performing a new action. Before dispatch, definitive failure/rejection can release reservations according to the pinned policy; after ambiguity only authenticated reconciliation can establish whether release is safe. Compensation remains a fresh independently authorized effect.

Then show altered supplier, excess budget, queue expiry, provisional-only evidence and reversal refusal. Finish with an unknown/query-unavailable case preserving the reservation and manual review. Re-run RFQ unchanged to demonstrate reuse rather than replacement.

## Exit evidence and decision

The eventual implementation must supply a run manifest with code/adapter/bundle/destination versions and hashes, executable non-skipping commands, seeds, host/OS, Node/Umbra/PostgreSQL configuration, single-writer boundary, controlled clock/skew policy, synthetic fixture/signing authority labels and admission real/stand-in status. Each matrix row records injection point, expected/observed before/after durable vector, destination key/digest/result/posting count, run count, outcome and artifact hashes. Store exact private canonical assertions and signatures securely; redact shareable traces and transport evidence. This design supplies no fictional commands for runtime components that do not yet exist.

Measure adapter-specific code/effort versus shared code changed, profile sizes and MPE-class fit, fault coverage, partial/reversal/ambiguity counts, approval cycle/handling time, unauthorized/replay rejection and reconciliation duration under declared synthetic conditions. A stable core plus two genuinely distinct profiles, rather than duplicated brokers or separate recovery implementations, supports the bounded reuse claim. Customer savings require an actual comparable baseline; partner/source integration and approval comprehension remain separate evaluation work.

Exit passes only when the shared transaction invariants, I01–I04/A01–A05/R01–R07 and canonical/UI agreement have reproducible non-skipping evidence for the declared sandbox. Report failures and unavailable stronger restore/source/authority capabilities as failed or blocked, never passed by mock substitution. Record one local durable disposition per business action and separate authenticated destination outcome evidence. The unavailable-destination fixture passes by refusing automatic effectful execution, not by pretending once-only semantics.

Recommend a constrained design-partner evaluation only with its source authentication, destination contract, human authority, privacy and recovery boundaries explicitly accepted. Otherwise repeat the blocked core work or commission the missing adapter/feasibility work. Preserve the cross-sprint admission dashboard: genuine content-bound proof, exact original fixed-slot/wire fit, <=4096-byte ceiling without enlarging fixed fields, <=10 ms verification on the specified VM, host/distributions, separate proving/witness costs and authoritative finalized-root/revocation evidence. These measurements are acceptance obligations, not results from this document. Production still requires genuine admission and authoritative roots, independent cryptographic/wire review, replicated opaque retention/receipts and operator economics, WAN/load/operations/key/restore hardening. Ledger effects additionally require signed authority, atomic replay/nullifier protection and CON-060 binding; none is established by this sandbox reuse demonstration.
