# Privacy contribution to the first three prototype sprints

This is an experiment plan, not implemented privacy evidence. Follow the [three-sprint sequence](sequence.md), using the real GossipSub harness and send-path instrumentation in the [transport contribution](transport.md). Start inexpensive falsification in the first few days; do not wait for durable recovery or genuine anonymous admission to find a plaintext selector or match-triggered request. The two-week sprint lengths are planning assumptions, not delivery commitments.

## Claim and observer boundary

The backend/desktop launch profile uses original symmetric MPE sealing, local keyed recognition and whole-shard reception. The bounded claim to test is: **changing only a receiver's private interests does not change infrastructure-visible subscription, carriage, control, retrieval or diagnostic behavior, while semantic event/profile identifiers remain private.** Explicitly authorized business publications are a separate application action with their own evidence and observable traffic cost.

An observer may operate a relay, gateway, retained-envelope service or their HTTP access/diagnostic infrastructure, and may inspect its decoded GossipSub RPCs, request/response frames, caches, databases and logs. Treat gateway and relay storage as outside the trusted workflow host. The trusted receiver may decrypt and maintain private domain indexes; this does not authorize exporting those indexes, match counters or plaintext failure diagnostics to infrastructure. Record deployment trust boundaries rather than labelling every PostgreSQL instance private.

Allowed visible metadata includes configured network/shard and wire/cryptographic/admission profile, fixed class/length, expiry and the selected admission fields; EIDs, complete-wire message IDs, connections and request schedules have their original protocol meanings. The salt, nonce and recognition tag form a **clear prefix inside the Sealed Body**, not encrypted metadata. Do not rewrite the envelope or claim these fields are hidden. Sealed bytes/EIDs can identify repeated carriage; their presence alone is not a semantic-ID leak. Public wire-profile selection is distinct from private `mpeprofile`, `mpecontract` and `dataschema` identities.

Keep event `source`, `type`, `(source,id)`, schema/contract references, correlations, business action IDs, signer/role, RFQ/invoice/approval labels and private subscriptions inside encryption. Do not map AsyncAPI logical operations to GossipSub topics or HTTP selectors. Resolve supported contracts from installed digest-pinned bundles; do not fetch event-supplied schema URIs or transitive `$ref`s. An authenticated unsupported contract may remain opaque for carriage and enter encrypted local quarantine; it must not trigger remote resolution, diagnostic upload or an effect.

This experiment does not establish ingress anonymity, resistance to global timing/volume correlation, forward secrecy, production admission or private mobile reception. Ingress still sees a direct client connection; selected shards and intentional publications remain observable. Local recognition CPU can affect scheduling, so measure timing residuals even though timing anonymity is outside this claim. OpenMLS/Signal identity, epoch, recovery and erasure work, PIR/OMR discovery/retrieval and Tor/Arti are deferred experiments. Do not claim symmetric stream confidentiality after key compromise.

## Shared harness and comparison rules

Prepare one immutable workload manifest: envelope bytes/hashes, publication schedule, full network/shard/profile, topology, clocks, expiry policy, random seeds, fault schedule, installed contracts, gateway options, cache limits and the admission adapter label. Preserve separate runs for the admission stand-in and any genuine verifier; a privacy pass with a stand-in grants no anonymous-membership assurance.

Use two private receiver configurations with equal key counts: A recognizes stream X, B recognizes stream Y. Replay exactly the same sealed workload and swap only those keys and local interests. Include a no-match receiver, no-op handlers, and subscriptions registered/cancelled/re-registered locally. Keep application actions disabled in the invariant pair. Confirm different recognition results inside trusted test instrumentation, while all selected-shard envelopes reach both configurations. Capture keys and plaintext fixture mappings only in a private ground-truth artifact, never relay telemetry or a published report.

Compare receiver-directed and receiver-originated traffic separately. Decode observations before Noise/TLS encryption at controlled endpoints as well as collecting packet totals; encrypted pcap alone cannot detect HTTP selector or protobuf-field leakage. Compare topic sets, RPC fields, IDONTWANT/IHAVE/IWANT behavior, HTTP path/query/header/body fields, page/window requests, destinations, retry/cancellation/ack actions, control counts, lengths, and bytes. Stable EIDs and full-wire hashes can correlate the fixed workload. Map ephemeral connection identifiers through the manifest and normalize only documented transport randomness; never normalize away interest-derived selectors, event-driven requests, page omissions or extra publications.

Use a controlled replay schedule for exact protocol-action assertions, then repeated live real-swarm runs to expose scheduler effects. Predeclare run count, seeds, capture completeness checks and the timing/noise assessment before execution. A planning minimum is five live pairs per selected scenario plus same-key A/A baseline pairs. Report raw differences, timing residuals and missing delivery denominators. Unexplained repeatable differences correlated with recognition fail the gate; ordinary live scheduling differences need causal investigation, not a claim of identical encrypted packets. No statistical non-detection establishes anonymity.

## Sprint 1 — cheap privacy falsification before integration grows

**Entry:** Agree the observer boundary, visible-metadata allowlist and private field list with transport/data-model owners. Pin the symmetric wire profile and the replaceable admission boundary. No durable store or MLS dependency is required.

**Ordered work:**

1. Review outbound topic construction, gateway routes, SDK subscription/cancellation, validation errors and logging defaults. Inventory every recognition-dependent callback and every network-capable component. Freeze the rule that local match, successful decrypt, unsupported schema and handler completion cannot automatically publish receipts or fetch objects.
2. Build small synthetic RFQ fixtures with unique canaries in private `source`, `type`, correlation, semantic profile/digest, schema URI, signer and action IDs. Add an unsupported contract with a canary URI and nested remote `$ref`, a malformed payload and an expired envelope. Use equal-size classes and independent sealing vectors; identical-class testing is not evidence that all size leakage disappears.
3. Feed them through local recognition/parser/SDK boundaries with network egress recorded or refused. Install only the known contract bundle. Scan HTTP requests, DNS attempts, serialized diagnostics, relay-visible cache/index keys and application logging for canaries, including URL/base64/JSON encodings. Compare a no-match run with a match run; trusted local results may differ, egress must not.
4. As soon as the real swarm exists, run one publisher, two receivers and one observing relay; decode outer RPCs. Confirm anonymous carriage fields and whole-shard topics as specified by transport, and confirm private operations/profile IDs never appear in them. If this dependency is unavailable, mark carriage evidence blocked rather than passing it from a mock.
5. In a test-only fault fixture, deliberately enable one match-triggered receipt and one schema fetch. Require the scanner/action comparator to detect both. Remove the fault before the clean run; record the sensitivity result separately.

**Demo:** The same envelope is recognized by one receiver and ignored by the other; both receive it. A known contract validates locally and an unknown contract stays locally quarantined. Neither path produces an acknowledgement, schema fetch or private diagnostic upload.

**Exit gate and report:** `evidence/sprint-1/privacy-boundary.md` records the metadata allowlist, trust diagram, egress inventory, canary cases, capture/scanner commands, injected-fault detections and clean results. Pass only with zero private-canary exposures, zero match-triggered egress, zero remote schema/ref resolution and a documented complete capture boundary. Static inspection alone is preliminary. A missed injected leak invalidates the clean result and blocks the next privacy claim.

## Sprint 2 — paired recognition traces across real carriage and caches

**Entry:** Sprint 1 canary/sensitivity gate passes, and the transport lane supplies actual anonymous GossipSub carriage and decoded control captures. Durable integration may proceed independently; it cannot waive this gate.

**Ordered work:**

1. Run the A/B interest swap on real Rust swarms for embedded and gateway receivers, one shard and eight shards, and all four wire classes. Replay the exact sealed bytes; keep equal key counts, ingress outcomes, topology, publications and application action schedule. Add duplicates, authenticated unknown profiles, locally failing handlers, expiry crossings, cancellation and backpressure. Local recognition/handler failure must not alter forwarding, scoring, subscriptions or IDONTWANT policy.
2. Capture HTTP and decoded GossipSub at clients, relay and gateway; include access/error logs, metrics labels, traces, cache keys and retained opaque rows. Scan for canaries and for derived private identifiers such as unhashed semantic IDs, their known digests and domain-labelled topic names. A hash of a private selector remains a selector; opaque naming is not a repair.
3. Compare fixed-schedule protocol actions and repeated live pairs using the shared rules. Keep all received and missing envelope counts alongside traffic totals so selective omission cannot masquerade as bandwidth improvement. Report recognition CPU by key count separately from admission verification CPU and measure any control/timing effects under pressure.
4. Inspect transport duplicate/history caches and gateway indexes after fill, eviction and expiry. Those may track original wire IDs under transport policy; they may not acquire decrypted stream names, matched-recipient labels or private profile fields. Check diagnostics emitted on invalid signatures, unknown contracts, overflow and handler failure, including exception text and telemetry exports.
5. Repeat the intentional receipt/fetch faults against this real capture path. Verify a detector catches changes even when the fields are encrypted and the leak is revealed only by action count, destination or schedule.

**Demo:** Swap X/Y keys without changing the relay's subscription/control/request decisions. Show privately that recognized event sets changed and publicly that the same selected-shard workload was carried. Show one detected fault and the repaired clean comparison.

**Exit gate and report:** `evidence/sprint-2/recognition-invariance.json` contains per-scenario manifest hashes, matched input/action comparisons, capture coverage, canary results, traffic totals, A/A baselines and unexplained differences. Accompany it with `recognition-invariance.md` explaining normalization, residuals and observer limitations. Pass only when the controlled action comparison agrees, no private metadata escapes, all sensitivity checks detect faults, and every live difference has evidence of a cause independent of recognition. A reproducible recognition-dependent protocol decision blocks reuse; a missing capture or unresolved difference is inconclusive, not a pass.

## Sprint 3 — durable backfill and deliberate business acknowledgements

**Entry:** Sprint 2 invariance passes; the recovery lane provides an authenticated durable inbox/outbox and crash-safe cursor, and the Store lane provides whole-window inventories with pagination. If either is absent, run the available carriage cases and label durable privacy blocked. SQLite/PostgreSQL persistence alone does not establish this protocol gate or replicated availability.

**Ordered work:**

1. Disconnect before a declared publication interval, reconnect, and recover the same shard/window in both key configurations. Match inventory/window/page size, cursor semantics, request order, retry/failover and expiry policy. Continue pagination across unrecognized pages; an empty local match set cannot stop it. Replay only retained envelopes permitted by expiry policy and record honest gaps without narrow requests for matched EIDs.
2. Crash before inbox commit, after commit before local handling, after local effect commit and during page/failover processing. Restore an older compatible checkpoint against newer durable dedup state. Local semantic processing can differ, but transport recovery progress must cover the same authenticated whole-shard inventory. Keep the feed/backfill cursor independent of a private handler's last matched event; never expose business checkpoints as gateway selectors.
3. Inspect store/gateway SQL logs, bound query parameters, rows/indexes, proxy access logs, replay caches and checkpoint exports after restart. Store opaque envelopes and allowed inventory metadata. Trusted workflow plaintext/dedup/signature evidence needs explicit local access/protection and no relay logging; quarantine stays encrypted. Include stale/unsupported schemas and remote-reference traps during replay, not just initial delivery.
4. Keep acknowledgements and attachment/object fetches disabled in the invariant recovery pair. An attachment reference stays inert data; do not auto-fetch it because decryption succeeds. Show that duplicates, replay, restore and handler completion create no implicit receipt.
5. Run a separate application-action scenario: an authorized actor explicitly sends a signed business acknowledgement after policy validates the exact event/action, role, domain and expiry. Record the authorization/action schedule and resulting sealed outbox publication. Reception, recognition or transport acceptance alone cannot authorize it. Replay/crash must preserve business action dedup and avoid generating another authorized action; any envelope retransmission retains its original identity and expiry. Include denied, revoked, changed-action and expired acknowledgement attempts.
6. Compare that explicit-action scenario against its declared application-action schedule, rather than folding it into the key-only invariant pair. The additional publication is intentional and may expose timing/volume correlation even with encrypted meaning; document this residual leakage. If fixed actions cannot run under a swapped receiver's authority, use identical authorized credentials/recognition for this separate test and state that limitation. Do not use a signed automatic match receipt as an exception.

**Demo:** Reconnect through multi-page recovery, including an unrecognized page, duplicate, expiry gap and source failover. Restart without changing page choices or producing acknowledgements. Then visibly authorize one business acknowledgement; show its separate outgoing traffic and deny invalid attempts.

**Exit gate and report:** `evidence/sprint-3/durable-privacy.md` and machine-readable comparison data tie each crash/page/failover case to captures, store/log scans, authenticated inventory coverage and action provenance. Pass only if whole-window recovery behavior is recognition-independent, no semantic selectors enter infrastructure state/logs, replay never resolves remote schemas or fetches matched objects, and every business acknowledgement has independent authorization evidence. Attach the raw manifests/captures privately and publish a redacted evidence index with hashes; do not publish key material or plaintext ground truth. A constrained pilot report must identify precisely which observer/profile/workload passed and which production gates remain red.

## Falsification and decisions

One attributable private semantic identifier in an HTTP/GossipSub selector, relay cache/database/log or exported diagnostic invalidates the confidentiality boundary. One recognition-triggered acknowledgement, fetch, changed control decision, narrowed inventory or early pagination stop invalidates recognition independence. One scanner blind spot demonstrated by an injected fault invalidates the claimed coverage. Intentional signed business actions are allowed only in the separate scenario with independent authority evidence; they cannot excuse hidden reception callbacks.

Keep failures visible and repair the responsible path before rerunning the affected scenarios. Do not repair bandwidth pressure with business topics or upstream interest filters. If whole-shard reception/recovery is too costly, report the measured operating limit and commission the deferred private-retrieval experiment. Passing these gates supplies bounded protocol-privacy evidence for the backend/desktop slice; it remains insufficient for ingress/timing anonymity, mobile budgets, MLS security, genuine finalized-root admission or production availability.
