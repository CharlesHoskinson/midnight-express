Filters and saved views

    Inbox

    Saved synthetic history. Reading and saving leave processing positions unchanged. A recorded review is a local disposition and authorizes no effect.

    Delivery tools

    Requested pause, current simulation authority, retained coverage and pending work are separate. Queue limits are two in-flight items, four queued events and 16 KiB per watch.

    Simulation controls

    Intake appends to the whole demo journal even without followers. Choose a feed in Directory first to simulate its declared source. Offline fixture checks are fixed reference evidence, not live verification.

    Folders

    Folders organize followed feeds locally. Deleting a folder moves its feeds to Unfiled and keeps their subscriptions.

    Optional Moth wallet · preprod

    Moth is an experimental, unaudited reference wallet. Connecting grants this origin wallet access beyond the connection-status calls used by this demo. It establishes no subscription permission or privacy proof.

    Disconnected. Local disconnect cannot revoke the origin grant; manage grants in Moth.

    Manage data

    IndexedDB stores bounded synthetic history, intents and organization on this browser. Storage is best effort and may be evicted. Reload restores history with delivery unchecked. It never restores wallet access or authenticated authority. Other sites on this shared GitHub Pages origin share its browser storage boundary. Use only synthetic material here; private consumer records need a dedicated origin and an authenticated local service.

    Reset simulation clears watches and history but retains folders and saved views. Clear history keeps watches paused with a fresh local start. Forget organization removes folders, pins and saved views without unfollowing. None revokes a Moth grant.
